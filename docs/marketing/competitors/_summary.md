# Competitor Landscape — Summary

_Generated 2026-09-27 · quick scan. Raw scrapes were taken with Firecrawl; no SEO-metrics (DataForSEO) connection, so traffic/backlink figures are omitted._

## Overview

The Saudi market for "checking what delivery apps pay restaurants" has one direct software competitor (Flowwtics, English-first, demo-led, SAR 399–1,299/mo) and one service competitor (Lift, Arabic, managed team via WhatsApp). Informational Arabic searches about commissions and compensations are answered by social posts, a few blogs (e.g. sec-intel.com on commissions) and news — not by any product. Globally, Voosh, Loop and Restaurant365 won this category with dispute-recovery content and case studies.

## Positioning Map

- Software ↔ Service: Tadqeeq, Flowwtics (software) … Lift (service).
- Arabic ↔ English: Tadqeeq, Lift (Arabic) … Flowwtics (English).
- Tadqeeq's open quadrant: **Arabic-first software** with auditable numbers.

## Key Takeaways

1. Nobody owns Arabic informational search for this problem — the biggest SEO opening.
2. Flowwtics' parameter-based Arabic pages are a structural SEO weakness Tadqeeq can beat with clean Arabic URLs.
3. Lift's "free audit" and "كم يوصلك من كل 100 ريال" hooks convert well; a free calculator/audit tool is the self-serve answer.
4. Real proof (recovered SAR, customer logos) is the main gap for all three — first case studies will stand out.

## Gaps & Opportunities

- Platform-specific Arabic guides: commission, fees, compensations, invoice format for HungerStation, Jahez, Keeta.
- Free "صافي المطعم من كل 100 ريال" calculator and invoice-check lead magnet.
- Comparison pages once live: "بديل Flowwtics", "تدقيق مقابل إدارة التطبيقات عبر وكالة".
